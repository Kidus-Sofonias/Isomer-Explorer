How to use it

Build a structure in four steps.

Start simple. The explorer will read formulas, IUPAC names, and common names, then move you to the result on smaller screens.

01 Enter a formula or name

Try C5H12, isobutane, pent-2-ene, toluene, or a supported IUPAC name.

02 Keep Family on Auto

Only switch family when you want to force alkane, alkene, alkyne, or aromatic results.

03 Tap Analyze

On phones, the page scrolls to the progress and result area so the response is obvious.

04 Inspect the card

Use Open for a larger view, or switch between atom labels and zig-zag bond-line mode.
