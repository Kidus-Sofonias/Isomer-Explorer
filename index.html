How to use

Explore isomers in style

1 Search for compounds

Enter a formula like C5H12 or a name like isobutane, toluene, or acetone.

2 Browse the results

Cards show each isomer with its IUPAC name, formula, and structure diagram. Switch between pages using the bottom tabs.

3 Flip & learn with Flashcards

Toggle flashcard mode to test yourself — tap a card to flip between the name and the structure diagram.

4 Guess before you see

Use the “Guess count” tool to predict how many isomers exist for a formula before the cards appear.

5 Daily formula & streak

Check the formula of the day and track how many unique compounds you explore each day.

6 Open for a closer look

Tap “Open” on any card for a full-screen view with atom labels, bond-line, or interactive 3D modes.
